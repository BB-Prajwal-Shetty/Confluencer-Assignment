# Zomato – Brand Voice Analysis

## Brand Voice Summary
- **Tone:** Witty, bold, humorous and conversational.
- **Target audience:** Young adults, students and food lovers.
- **Content themes:** Food cravings, offers, memes and trending topics.
- **Communication style:** Short, catchy lines with relatable humour and emojis.

## Sample Social Posts
1. That 2 AM craving has only one solution. 🍕 #MidnightCravings #Zomato
2. Good food = Good mood. No arguments! ❤️ #FoodLove
3. Your weekend plans? We suggest food first. 😋 #WeekendVibes

## Approach
I identified recurring tone, audience cues, content themes and writing patterns from publicly visible brand communication. I then used those characteristics to draft short, original sample posts.

**Tools:** ChatGPT for analysis and drafting; Google Forms for collecting brand inputs; Zapier for automating the workflow.

*These are original sample posts inspired by the brand's general style, not official Zomato posts.*
