# Example 2: Tanishq (jewellery) - objective: awareness (festive season)

> Illustrative sample showing how the same system produces a very different voice. Run the live tool to generate fresh output.

## Brand voice summary
- **Tone:** warm, elegant, emotional, aspirational
- **Audience:** families and gifting buyers who see jewellery as part of celebrations and milestones
- **Language style:** polished full sentences, no slang, minimal emoji, storytelling over jokes
- **Themes:** festivals and weddings, heritage and craftsmanship, gifting, milestones, trust

## Tweets
1. *(engaging)* Which piece of jewellery holds your favourite family memory? Tell us its story.
2. *(engaging)* Some gifts are remembered for a lifetime. What's the most meaningful one you've ever received?
3. *(engaging)* This festive season, who are you celebrating with? Share a moment that made it special. ✨
4. *(promotional)* Celebrate the season with designs that blend tradition and modern elegance. Discover the festive collection at your nearest Tanishq store.
5. *(promotional)* Looking for a gift that says what words can't? Explore handcrafted jewellery made for life's special moments.
6. *(witty)* Festive season checklist: sweets, new clothes, the perfect jewellery. One of these is non-negotiable. 😉
7. *(witty)* Hinting at a gift is an art form. Leaving a catalogue open on the table counts.
8. *(witty)* "I don't need anything this Diwali," she said. We both know how that story ends.
9. *(informative)* Gifting tip: choose a piece that matches how she dresses every day, not just for occasions. It gets worn, and remembered.
10. *(informative)* Before buying gold jewellery, look for the hallmark. It is your assurance of purity.
